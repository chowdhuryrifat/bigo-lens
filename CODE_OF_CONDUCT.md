# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want BigO Lens to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, especially in code review;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users;
- being honest about results, including test failures and limitations.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private;
- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- publishing others' private information without their explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and
when an individual officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainers through GitHub, for example by contacting the repository
owner listed in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with
discretion. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors whose behaviour violates this Code of Conduct.
